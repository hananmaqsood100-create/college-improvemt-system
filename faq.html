Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our system

The Campus Feedback System is a web-based platform designed to collect, manage, and analyze feedback from students and staff about college services, infrastructure, and policies. It ensures complete anonymity while helping college management improve the campus experience.

Yes, 100% anonymous! Your identity is never revealed. We collect feedback without tracking personal information unless you choose to provide an email for follow-up.

Click on 'Register' button and fill in your details using your college email address. Select your user type (Student/Faculty/Staff) and create a password. Verify your email and you're ready to submit feedback!

No, registration is required to prevent spam and abuse. However, your identity is completely protected even after registration.

You can provide feedback on: Infrastructure & Facilities, Academic Quality, Faculty & Staff, Policies & Management, Campus Services, and Others. Choose the category that best fits your feedback.

After logging in, click 'Submit Feedback'. Fill in the form with your feedback details, rate your experience, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation message.

Feedback cannot be edited after submission to maintain data integrity. However, you can submit additional feedback if needed.

Click 'Forgot Password' on the login page and enter your email. We'll send you instructions to reset your password.
